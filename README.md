XAKARTA

"XAKARTA" is an action-adventure game that places players in an explorable 3D world where they can face enemies in combat. During exploration, players can upgrade their equipment and weapons.
The game's ultimate goal is to reach the final stage of the area, where players will encounter a boss to further their progress. This game was created for entertainment purposes.
